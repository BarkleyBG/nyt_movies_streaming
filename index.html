About This Site

This site helps you find where to stream every Academy Award Best Picture nominee from the 21st century. Browse nominees and winners by ceremony year, filter by streaming service, and track your progress.

Switch to the NYT Top 100 tab to browse the New York Times' 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century.

How to Use

  • Switch tabs: Use the tab bar to toggle between the NYT Top 100 list and Oscar Best Picture nominees.
  • Filter by service: Click one or more streaming service pills (Netflix, Hulu, etc.) to see movies available on those platforms. Click All to clear the selection.
  • Mark as seen: Click "Seen" on any movie to track your progress. Click again to undo.
  • Want to see: Click "Want to See" to build your personal watchlist.
  • Search: Type a title, director, or year into the search box to narrow results.
  • Sort: Use the dropdown to sort by rank, title, year, or rating.
  • Show filters: Toggle between All, Unseen Only, Want to See, Already Seen, or Winners Only (Oscar tab).

Your seen/watchlist data is saved locally in your browser and will persist between visits.

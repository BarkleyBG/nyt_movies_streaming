About This Site

This site helps you find where to stream the movies from The New York Times' "Best Movies of the 21st Century" list. Filter by streaming service, track which films you've seen, and build your watchlist — all in one place.

How to Use

  • Filter by service: Click a streaming service pill (Netflix, Hulu, etc.) to see only movies available on that platform.
  • Mark as seen: Click "Seen" on any movie to track your progress. Click again to undo.
  • Want to see: Click "Want to See" to build your personal watchlist.
  • Search: Type a title, director, or year into the search box to narrow results.
  • Sort: Use the dropdown to sort by rank, title, or release year.
  • Show filters: Toggle between All, Unseen Only, Want to See, or Already Seen.

Your seen/watchlist data is saved locally in your browser and will persist between visits.
